Digital vs Printed Kankotri

Most families no longer choose one or the other. Here's an honest look at both, and how to combine them.

VS

At a glance

Feels traditional✓ Strongest✓ With blessings and Hindi wording

When printed still matters

For grandparents, the family guru and the closest elders, a hand-delivered card carries respect that a message cannot. Many families also keep a printed kankotri as a keepsake.

The hybrid approach

  1. Print a small number of cards for elders and close family.
  2. Add the BFI QR code on the printed card so every function and map is one scan away.
  3. Share the digital invitation with everyone else on WhatsApp.
  4. Send a reminder with the same link a week before the wedding.

What to put on each

Keep the printed card short: blessing, names, families and the main wedding date. Put the full list of functions, timings, venues, dress codes and RSVP on the digital invitation, where it can be updated.

Questions

Is a digital kankotri cheaper than printing?

For large guest lists and outstation guests it usually is, because there is no printing or courier. Many families still print a few cards for elders.

Can I add a QR code to my printed card?

Yes, every BFI package includes a QR code you can print on your cards.
