Rajasthani motifs and what they mean

  • Jharokha: the carved window, a frame for the couple's names
  • Gaj (elephant): auspicious, a symbol of welcome and prosperity
  • Mor (peacock): grace and love
  • Leheriya and bandhej: the colours of Teej, Gangaur and celebration
  • Haveli and fort skylines: the grandeur of Rajput and Marwari heritage

Designs in our collection

Rajwada Royale and Sheesh Mahal bring palace grandeur; Gajraj Swagat welcomes guests with elephants; Mor Pankh uses the peacock; Chhatri is a minimal take on the Rajasthani pavilion.

Matching your city

Jaipur weddings often lean pink and gold; Jodhpur's blue city suits navy and indigo, as in Neel Mahal; Udaipur's lakes suit soft pastels; Shekhawati families often love fresco-inspired detail. We can tune colours on Premium and Luxury.
