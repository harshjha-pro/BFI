Orders and payment

Package prices are shown on the pricing page and each design page. Work starts after a 50% advance; the balance is due after you approve the final invitation and before the final link is shared.

Delivery

Delivery times (24, 48 or 72 hours) start once we have your complete details. We tell you in advance if a date is not possible.

Changes

One round of changes is included in every package; spelling corrections are always free. Further changes or extra pages are quoted before we make them.

Cancellations and refunds

If you cancel before we share the first preview, we refund the advance minus any work already done. After the first preview, the advance is not refundable.

Your content

You confirm you have the right to use the names, photos and music you send. We may show a design in our portfolio only with your permission and never with your personal details.

Link availability

Invitation links stay live through the wedding and for at least 6 months after.
