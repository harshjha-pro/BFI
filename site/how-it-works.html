Questions

Anything else? A designer answers on WhatsApp within an hour.

Ask on WhatsApp
How long does it take?

Basic invitations are ready in 24 hours, Premium in 48 hours and Luxury in 72 hours after we receive your complete details.

Can I change details after it's shared?

Yes. Your link stays the same, so guests always see the latest version. One round of changes is free; spelling fixes are always free.

Is it available in Hindi?

Choose English, Hindi, Marwari + Hindi or bilingual. We add traditional blessings like ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ on request.

How do guests receive it?

You get a private link, a ready-to-forward WhatsApp message and a QR code you can print on physical cards.

Do you collect RSVPs?

Yes, on Premium and Luxury: guests confirm attendance and guest count, and you see the list.

How do I pay?

We confirm the package on WhatsApp and share a UPI or bank link. 50% to start, 50% after you approve.

What if I don't like the first preview?

Tell us what to change. One full round of changes is included, and we can switch to another design of the same package.
