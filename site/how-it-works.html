How It Works

Get your digital wedding invitation in four simple steps.

1. Choose a Design

Browse our curated collection or ask for a bespoke design.

2. Customize

Share names, functions, venues, language and music.

3. Preview

Review the invitation on your phone; one round of changes included.

4. Share

Send your link and QR code to every guest on WhatsApp.

What we need from you

  • Bride's and groom's names (and how you'd like them written)
  • Parents', grandparents' and elders' names
  • Every function: date, time, venue, Google Maps link
  • Language, blessing line and music choice
  • Photos (optional, for Premium and Luxury)

Questions

How long does it take?

Basic invitations are ready in 24 hours, Premium in 48 hours and Luxury in 72 hours after we receive your details.

Can I change details after it's shared?

Yes. Your link stays the same, so guests always see the latest version. One round of changes is free; spelling fixes are always free.

Is it in Hindi?

Choose English, Hindi, Marwari + Hindi or bilingual. We add traditional blessings like ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ on request.

How do guests receive it?

You get a private link, a WhatsApp-ready message and a QR code for printed cards.

Do you collect RSVPs?

Yes, optionally: guests confirm attendance and guest count, and you see the list.

How do I pay?

We confirm the package with you on WhatsApp and share a UPI or bank payment link. 50% to start, 50% on approval.
