How RSVP works

  1. Guests open your invitation and tap RSVP.
  2. They enter their name, whether they're attending and the number of guests.
  3. Optionally, they choose a meal preference.
  4. You receive the responses and can share totals with your caterer, hotel and transport team.

Why it helps Indian weddings

  • Large guest lists across many functions are hard to track by phone.
  • Caterers and hotels need numbers days in advance.
  • Outstation and NRI guests can reply in one tap from anywhere.

Set a deadline

Most families ask for RSVPs one to two weeks before the wedding. Send a reminder on WhatsApp with the same link a few days before the deadline.

Questions

Which packages include RSVP?

Premium and Luxury. Basic invitations include a contact number instead.

Can guests change their RSVP?

Yes, they can send it again with updated details.

Is guest information private?

Yes. Responses are only for you and the BFI team; we never share them.
