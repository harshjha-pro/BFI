Pricing Questions

Need a custom quote for several functions or family weddings? We'll reply within an hour.

Get a quoteSend an enquiry
Are there any hidden charges?

No. The package price covers design, your invitation website, the share link and QR code, and one round of changes. Extra rounds or extra pages are quoted before we start.

How do I pay?

On WhatsApp we confirm your package and share a UPI or bank transfer link. You pay 50% to start and 50% after you approve the final invitation.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can move from Basic to Premium or Luxury at any time; you only pay the difference.

How long does the invitation link stay live?

Your link stays live through your wedding and for at least 6 months after, so guests can revisit the photos and details.

Do you offer a discount for multiple events or family weddings?

Message us on WhatsApp. For sibling weddings or extra functions we quote a combined price.
