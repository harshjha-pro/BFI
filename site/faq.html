Marwari invitations

What is a Marwari kankotri?

A kankotri is the traditional Marwari wedding invitation. It opens with a blessing to Lord Ganesha, names the couple with both families and elders, and lists each function, from Ganesh Sthapana and Mayra to Phere. A digital kankotri carries the same content as a mobile invitation website.

What should a Marwari wedding invitation include?

An opening blessing, the couple's names, both sets of parents (often grandparents too), each function with date, time and venue, an RSVP contact and a line of welcome. See our wording guide.

How can I create a digital Marwari wedding invitation?

Choose a design, share names, functions and venues through our online form or on WhatsApp, review the preview on your phone, then share the final link and QR code.

Can a digital invitation include Mayra and Pithi?

Yes. Add any function your family celebrates, including Mayra (Bhaat), Pithi, Mahila Sangeet, Nikasi and Toran, each with its own date, time, venue and notes.

Can wedding invitations be written in Hindi?

Yes. Choose English, Hindi, Marwari with Hindi, or bilingual. Many families use Hindi for the blessing and elders' names and English for friends and NRI guests.

What is the difference between a video invitation and an invitation website?

A video plays once and cannot be updated. An invitation website stays open on the guest's phone, has a separate entry for every function with maps, can collect RSVPs and can be updated without re-sending.

Features

Can I include multiple wedding events?

Yes. Basic includes up to 3 functions, Premium up to 8, and Luxury has no limit.

How does RSVP work on a wedding invitation website?

On Premium and Luxury, guests tap RSVP on the invitation, choose whether they are attending and how many people are coming. You receive the responses, so you can share numbers with your caterer and hotel.

Can I share the invitation through WhatsApp?

Yes. You receive a private link and a ready-to-forward WhatsApp message. The link opens directly in the phone's browser, with no app to install.

Can I add Google Maps directions?

Yes. Every function can have its own Google Maps link, so guests get directions in one tap.

Do you provide a QR code?

Yes, every package includes a QR code you can print on physical cards or show at the venue.

Ordering, delivery and payment

How long does it take to create a wedding invitation website?

Basic 24 hours, Premium 48 hours and Luxury 72 hours, counted from when we have all your details and the advance.

How long does the invitation link remain active?

Through your wedding and for at least 6 months after it.

How do I pay?

50% advance by UPI or bank transfer to start, and the remaining 50% after you approve the preview, before the final link is delivered.

What does one free revision mean?

After your first preview, send all your changes together in one message and we apply them. Spelling and name corrections are always free.

Can I cancel?

If you cancel before the first preview, we refund the advance minus any work already done. After the first preview the advance is not refundable. See terms.
