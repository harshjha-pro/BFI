Our Work › Mahi & Ayush

Client project · Engagement invitation website

Mahi & Ayush

Chittorgarh, Rajasthan · November 2026 · Custom design

A soft, storybook-style engagement invitation with an illustrated opening, family blessings and a scroll-through celebration.

Open the live invitation ↗All work

Best viewed on a phone.

What we built

  • Illustrated opening with a dove and sealed scroll; sound on or skip
  • Blessings: ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ and ॥ राधे कृष्ण ॥
  • Scroll-animated illustrated story and handwritten-style headings
  • Our story and a memories gallery
  • Programme: welcome breakfast, ring ceremony and lunch with timings
  • Live countdown to the ceremony
  • Venue map with one-tap directions and copy address
  • Interactive storybook, 'The Story Behind the Yes'
  • Background music with a play / pause control
  • Custom monogram, WhatsApp sharing, copy link and a link-preview card

The design

Blush blossoms, ivory paper and fine gold line ornaments, with script names and serif details: calm and romantic rather than heavy and ornate.
