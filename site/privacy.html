Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

BFI · Marwari Invitations ("BFI", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Details you send in enquiry and customize forms: names, phone, email, wedding date, city, functions, venues and messages.
  • Account details if you create an account: name, email, phone, saved drafts, favourites and reviews.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the website securely.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and make your invitation.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp, phone or email about your order.
  • We never sell your data and never use it for unrelated advertising.

Your invitation

Your invitation is published at a private link. Anyone you share the link with can open it. Ask us at any time to take it down.

Storage

Data is stored with our hosting providers (Supabase and Vercel) using encrypted connections. Only you and the BFI team can see your enquiries and drafts.
