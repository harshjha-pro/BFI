Marwari wedding rituals, in order

Customs vary by family and region, so always check with your elders. This is the order most Marwari weddings follow.

  1. Sagai / Roka

    The formal engagement: families exchange blessings, gifts and shagun, and the wedding is fixed.

  2. Ganesh Puja (Vinayak Sthapna)

    The first ritual at both homes: Lord Ganesha is invited so the wedding happens without obstacles. Most Marwari invitations open with ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ for this reason.

  3. Mayra (Bhaat)

    The bride's or groom's maternal uncle (mama) and his family arrive with clothes, jewellery and gifts for the mother and the family. It is usually a separate function on the invitation.

  4. Pithi / Haldi

    A paste of turmeric, gram flour and oil is applied to the bride and groom by family women, with folk songs.

  5. Mehendi

    Henna for the bride and the women of the family, often with the groom's initials hidden in the design.

  6. Mahila Sangeet

    An evening of songs and dance led by the women of the family; today often a full sangeet night.

  7. Nikasi and Baraat

    The groom, dressed in sehra and safa, leaves on a mare after his sister-in-law and family perform rituals. The baraat travels to the wedding venue with music.

  8. Toran and Varmala

    At the bride's door the groom touches the toran (decorated arch) with a neem branch or sword, then the couple exchange garlands.

  9. Phere

    The couple take seven rounds around the sacred fire, each with a vow. This is the main wedding ceremony and usually the main date on the invitation.

  10. Vidaai and Griha Pravesh

    The emotional farewell of the bride, followed by her welcome into the groom's home.

Invitation send-out checklist

  • 8–10 weeks before: finalise the guest list and the list of functions with your elders.
  • 6–8 weeks before: choose a design and send names, dates and venues (double-check spellings of elders' names).
  • 4–8 weeks before: share the invitation link on WhatsApp; post printed cards with the QR code to elders.
  • 2 weeks before: check RSVPs and guest counts; share with your caterer and hotel.
  • 1 week before: send a reminder with the same link; update any timing changes.

Questions families ask

When should we send a Marwari wedding invitation?

Send it 4–8 weeks before the wedding (8–12 weeks for NRI guests), and a reminder on WhatsApp about one week before. A digital link makes the reminder easy.

What should a Marwari wedding invitation include?

An opening blessing, the couple's names, both sets of parents (and often grandparents), the list of functions with date, time and venue, the RSVP contact, and a line of welcome. Many families also list the Mayra and Mahila Sangeet separately.

Which events go on the invitation?

Usually Ganesh Puja, Mayra, Pithi/Haldi, Mehendi, Mahila Sangeet, Baraat, Phere and the Reception. Close-family rituals are often shared on a separate page for family only.

Should the invitation be in Hindi or English?

Many families choose bilingual: Hindi for elders and the blessing, English for friends and NRI guests. BFI supports English, Hindi, Marwari + Hindi and bilingual.
