What is a wedding invitation website?

It is a small, private website made only for your wedding. Guests open it from a WhatsApp link or a QR code, and it works like an app on their phone without installing anything.

Unlike a picture card, it can hold as many functions as your family celebrates, and it can be updated if a time or venue changes.

What a Marwari invitation website usually contains

  1. Cover with the opening blessing (॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥) and the couple's names
  2. Blessings from grandparents and elders
  3. Both families: the bride's and groom's parents, written the way your family prefers
  4. Functions: Ganesh Sthapana, Mayra, Pithi, Mehendi, Mahila Sangeet, Baraat, Phere, Reception, each with date, time, venue and dress code
  5. Venue directions with Google Maps
  6. RSVP so guests confirm attendance and numbers (Premium and Luxury)
  7. Gallery, countdown and music (Premium and Luxury)

Why families choose a website over a PDF or video

  • A PDF is hard to read on a phone and cannot be updated.
  • A video plays once, and guests cannot tap to get directions.
  • A website keeps every function, map and RSVP in one place and stays live after the wedding.

How to get one made

Choose a design from the collection, share your names and functions through our form or on WhatsApp, review the preview on your phone, then share the link. It takes 24–72 hours depending on the package.

Questions

Do guests need to install an app?

No. The invitation opens in the phone's browser from a link or QR code.

Can I keep some functions for family only?

Yes. Tell us which functions are for close family and we will mark them or keep them on a separate page.
