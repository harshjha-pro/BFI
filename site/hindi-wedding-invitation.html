Common lines in a Hindi invitation

  • ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥: the opening invocation
  • सादर आमंत्रण: “a respectful invitation”
  • शुभ विवाह: the auspicious wedding
  • स्वागतोत्सुक: family members eager to welcome you, often listed at the end
  • दर्शनाभिलाषी: those who look forward to your presence, often children of the family

Honorifics

  • चि. (Chiranjeevi) before the groom's name
  • सौ. कां. (Saubhagyakankshini) before the bride's name
  • श्रीमती / श्री for parents and elders; स्व. for late family members

Hindi, English or bilingual?

Many families choose bilingual: Hindi for the blessing, elders and families, and English for function details so younger and NRI guests can read easily. We set Devanagari in a proper Hindi typeface so it looks as good as the English.
