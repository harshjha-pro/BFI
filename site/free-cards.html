Wedding invitations Ask on WhatsApp
Is the card maker really free?

Yes. Choose a template, type your details and download the card as an image or share it on WhatsApp. No sign-up and no watermark on your text.

Which occasions are included?

16 templates: wedding, save the date, Sagai, anniversary, kids' and adult birthdays, Godh Bharai, Naamkaran, Mundan, Griha Pravesh, puja or bhajan sandhya, Diwali, Holi, Teej and Gangaur, retirement and prayer meet.

Can I download a PDF to print?

Yes. Download as PNG, JPG or a print-ready PDF. Choose Card size for printing, Status size for WhatsApp or Instagram stories, or Square for posts. You can also download any template blank, without text.

Can I write in Hindi?

Yes. Type in Hindi or English; Devanagari is supported in every text box.

Can BFI make a full invitation website for my event?

Yes. For weddings and big family functions we make a full invitation website with every event, maps, music and RSVP. See pricing.
