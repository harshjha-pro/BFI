How to Invite Guests to Mayra, Pithi and Phere

Not every guest is invited to every function. Here's how Marwari families usually decide, and how to show it clearly on one invitation.

Mayra · familyPithi · familyPhere · everyone

Who usually attends which function

FunctionUsually for
Ganesh SthapanaClose family
Mayra (Bhaat)Family, and the maternal side who bring the mayra
Pithi / HaldiFamily and close relatives, often women of the family
Mehendi & Mahila SangeetWomen of the family and friends; many families now invite everyone
Baraat & PhereAll invited guests
ReceptionAll invited guests, colleagues and friends

Customs differ by family and region; always follow your elders.

Showing family-only functions on one invitation

  • Mark close-family functions with a small note such as “Family only”.
  • Or create a separate family page, shared only with relatives.
  • Keep the public invitation to the functions everyone attends.

Wording that works

For family functions: “With the blessings of our elders, we invite our family to the Mayra ceremony.”

For everyone: “Your presence at the Phere and Reception will make our joy complete.”

Timing

Share the invitation 4–8 weeks before the wedding. Send the family page when the Mayra and Pithi dates are fixed, and a reminder to everyone a week before.

Questions

Can one invitation have different functions for different guests?

Yes. We can mark family-only functions or make a separate family page.

Should the Mayra be on the invitation?

Many families list it, especially for relatives who travel. Some keep it for the family page only.
