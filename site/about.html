About BFI

Preserving Marwari Traditions in a Digital Way

BFI · Marwari Invitations is a digital invitation studio in Jaipur, Rajasthan, that designs wedding invitations only for Marwari families.

We blend Marwari customs, royal Rajasthani art and modern convenience: from the first ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ to the last reception dinner, every function and every elder's name is in its right place.

Our story

From kankotri to your phone

Every Marwari wedding begins with a kankotri: an invitation carried with care to every relative. Families now share invitations on WhatsApp, but most digital cards forget the rituals, the elders and the language that make a Marwari wedding what it is.

BFI was started to fix that: invitations that honour every ritual, list every elder correctly and still feel effortless for guests to open.

Our heritage

Inspired by Rajasthan

Shekhawati havelis, jharokha windows, royal elephants, peacocks and the colours of Gangaur: our designs carry the same craft, made for the screens your family uses every day.
