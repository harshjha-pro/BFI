Questions

Invitation video FAQ

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

What is an invitation video?

A short animated video with your blessing, names, families, functions and venues, set to music. Guests watch it on WhatsApp or Instagram, and you can share it as a Status.

What format will I receive?

A vertical MP4 video (9:16), the size that fits WhatsApp, Instagram Reels and Status.

How long does it take?

Basic in 2 days, Premium in 3 days and Luxury in 4 days, counted from when we have your complete details and the 50% advance.

Can the video be in Hindi?

Yes: Hindi, English or bilingual. Traditional lines such as ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ can be added.

Can I have a video and an invitation website?

Yes. Many families send the video first and the website link for maps, RSVP and details. Ask on WhatsApp for both together.

How do I pay?

50% to start and 50% after you approve the video. One round of changes is included.
