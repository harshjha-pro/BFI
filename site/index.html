Our approach

Rooted in tradition. Made for today.

A Marwari invitation carries more than a date. It carries the blessing that opens it, the elders whose names come first, and every function your family celebrates, from Ganesh Sthapana to Phere.

We set each name in the order your family follows, write in Hindi, English or both, and check every detail by hand before you see a preview. The result opens on any phone, in one tap, from WhatsApp.

Marwari heritage

Every ritual has its place

Customs differ between families and regions. You choose which functions appear, in your order and your wording.

  1. 01Ganesh Sthapana

    The first invitation goes to Ganpati ji

  2. 02Mayra

    The mama's family arrives with gifts

  3. 03Pithi

    Turmeric, songs and family blessings

  4. 04Mehendi

    Henna and the groom's hidden initials

  5. 05Mahila Sangeet

    An evening led by the women of the family

  6. 06Baraat & Nikasi

    The groom rides out with music

  7. 07Toran & Varmala

    At the bride's door, garlands exchanged

  8. 08Phere

    Seven vows around the sacred fire

What is a digital Marwari wedding invitation?

A mobile invitation website for your wedding: couple and family names, every function with date, time and Google Maps, plus music, photos and RSVP on higher packages. Guests open it from a WhatsApp link or a QR code.

How much does it cost?

Basic from ₹3,999, Premium from ₹6,999 and Luxury from ₹11,999. You pay 50% to start and 50% after you approve. See pricing.

How fast will it be ready?

Basic in 24 hours, Premium in 48 hours and Luxury in 72 hours, counted from when we have all your details and the advance.

Can it include Mayra, Pithi and other rituals?

Yes. Add any function: Ganesh Sthapana, Mayra, Pithi, Mehendi, Mahila Sangeet, Baraat, Phere, Reception or your own family rituals.

Can the invitation be in Hindi or Marwari?

Yes: English, Hindi, Marwari with Hindi, or bilingual, with traditional lines like ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥.

Can I see it before paying the balance?

Yes. You review a private preview on your phone, get one round of changes, and pay the remaining 50% only after you approve.
