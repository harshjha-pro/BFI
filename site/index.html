Free: Marwari Wedding Guide
Rituals in order, invitation wording in Hindi & English, and a send-out checklist.
What is a digital wedding invitation?

It is a beautiful mobile invitation website for your wedding: your names, both families, every function with date, time and Google Maps, plus music, photos and RSVP. Guests open it from a WhatsApp link or a QR code on a printed card.

How much does a digital Marwari wedding invitation cost at BFI?

Packages start at ₹3,999 (Basic), ₹6,999 (Premium) and ₹11,999 (Luxury). You pay 50% to begin and 50% after you approve the final invitation. See the pricing page.

How fast will my invitation be ready?

Basic in 24 hours, Premium in 48 hours and Luxury in 72 hours after we receive your complete details.

Can you write the invitation in Hindi or Marwari?

Yes. Choose English, Hindi, Marwari with Hindi, or bilingual. We add traditional lines such as ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ on request.

Can I change details after sharing the link?

Yes. The link stays the same, so guests always see the latest version. One round of changes is free and spelling fixes are always free.

Do you serve families outside Rajasthan and NRIs?

Yes. Everything happens on WhatsApp, so we work with Marwari families anywhere in India and abroad.
