# Reference study: thedigitalyes.com (18 November 2026)

The owner asked: *why does our site feel boring when The Digital Yes feels lively, and how do we follow it?*

The Digital Yes is a European digital wedding invitation studio (36 designs, from €175). Their homepage is about 19,000 px long, and I counted 111 running animations, 306 images and 2 videos. Ours is about 8,000 px long, with 1 animation, 9 images and no video.

**Their palette and type are almost the same as ours:**

- Palette: ivory, burgundy, champagne gold.
- Type: Playfair Display italic, Cormorant Garamond and Montserrat.

So the difference isn't colour. It's **imagery, motion and interaction**.

## Why ours feels flat (and theirs doesn't)

| # | The Digital Yes | Bfiluxe today | The fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The product is always on a phone.** Every design is shown as a real phone mockup, often beside a printed card or envelope, on a soft beige "studio" background. | Flat cover images in thin boxes. | Phone mockups (with a printed card or envelope beside them) for all 8 designs. |
| 2 | **Lush illustrated artwork** (watercolour venues, florals, chandeliers, wax seals). Every card is different. | Our covers are calmer and more alike; mostly names on a plain background. | Richer cover art per design: haveli, jharokha, elephants, marigolds, peacock, mehendi. |
| 3 | **Hero with floating 3D-style objects** (rings, a key with a tassel, flowers, a villa) around a phone. Feels playful. | A static boxed card next to the text. | Hero phone running our live demo, with floating Marwari objects: kalash, marigold toran, wax seal, rings, diya, a bandhej pouch. |
| 4 | **Moving carousel of phones** (fanned and staggered heights) right under the hero. | No carousel. | A gently scrolling fan of our 8 designs plus the 5 live openings. |
| 5 | **Interactive "Inside your invitation"**: click a section (Countdown, Scratch, Map, Dress code, RSVP) and the phone screen changes. | A text list beside a phone. | Same pattern using our real demo screens: Ganesh line, scratch, countdown, map, RSVP. |
| 6 | **Scroll-driven storytelling**: a sticky "How it works" where the phone changes as you scroll, and a large pull-quote that fills with colour as you read. | A static 4-step grid. | A sticky phone for the four steps, plus one large Hindi/English pull-quote ("॥ शुभ विवाह ॥ The memory starts the second they open it") that reveals on scroll. |
| 7 | **Shop-like product grid**: NEW / BEST SELLER badges, hearts, "From €175", tabs (Classic / 3D Pop-up). | Plain cards with two buttons each. | Badges (only honest ones, such as "New" or "Hindi + English"), a "From ₹…" price, and tabs (Websites / Videos / Free cards). |
| 8 | **Human proof**: an atelier video of a designer drawing on an iPad, on a dark burgundy rounded panel. | No people and no video. | A short behind-the-scenes clip: Hindi names being typed into a design, a hand checking wording. Film it on a phone. |
| 9 | **A before/after** (Essential versus Premium redrawn around your venue). | A price table only. | Basic versus Luxury, side by side on two phones. |
| 10 | **Playful touches**: a gift card that tilts with the cursor, illustrated stickers (couple, swans, rings, envelope) floating around "You'll love it." | None. | A gift voucher card that tilts, and Marwari stickers (a couple in a safa and lehenga, an elephant, a doli, rings) around our closing call to action. |
| 11 | **Urgency**: a top promotion bar and a Black Friday pop-up. | None. | A light top bar for real offers only, such as the launch on 21 November or wedding-season slots. |
| 12 | **Big numbers**: 0.0 rating, 3,000+ couples, 190,000+ followers. | We have none yet. | **Do not invent numbers.** Use facts we can stand behind: "8 designs · 5 live openings · Hindi & English · ready in 24–72 hours". |

## What we must not copy

- Their designs, illustrations, photos, wording, product names or layout pixel for pixel. That is their work.
- We copy **patterns** (phone mockups, motion, interaction, layout rhythm) and fill them with our own Marwari art and words.
- Their review and follower numbers. We show real proof only once we have it.

## Plan

1. **The look of the product (biggest win):**
   - phone mockups and printed-card mockups for all 8 designs;
   - a richer illustrated cover for each design;
   - a set of Marwari illustrated objects and stickers.
2. **Homepage rebuild:**
   - floating-object hero;
   - fanned phone carousel;
   - shop-style grid with badges;
   - an interactive "Inside your invitation" phone;
   - a sticky how-it-works;
   - a scroll-reveal pull-quote;
   - a burgundy atelier video panel;
   - a before/after;
   - a tilting gift voucher;
   - sticker call to action.
3. **The same system on the other pages** (invitations, design pages, pricing, videos).
4. **Social:** reuse the same phone mockups, stickers and illustrated objects on Instagram, so the feed and the website look like one brand.
