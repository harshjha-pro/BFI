# Bfiluxe social media launch plan

Written 17 November 2026 for the new Bfiluxe Instagram account. It builds on the owner's ChatGPT conversation (brand Bfiluxe; champagne gold and ivory; "Invitations Made Extraordinary"; Metricool for scheduling; logo later), on the live website, and on current Instagram guidance.

## 1. Positioning: what people should remember

**One line:** *Bfiluxe makes invitations that guests open, not just forward.*

- **What we sell:**
  - invitation websites (₹3,999 / ₹6,999 / ₹11,999);
  - invitation videos (₹1,499 / ₹2,499 / ₹3,999);
  - a free card maker, which brings people in.
- **Who it's for, first:** Marwari and North Indian families planning a wedding in the next 2 to 6 months, and the cousins and sisters who actually do the invitation work.
- **Why us:** cinematic openings (wax seal, jharokha doors, diya), every ritual in the right order, elders' names checked by hand, and Hindi or English.
- **What we never post:** fake reviews, fake client counts or "1000+ couples" style claims. Until real reviews exist, show the work, the process and the prices.

## 2. The premium look (consistency is what reads as "luxury")

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Colours | Ivory `#F7F3EB`, champagne gold `#B18C58`, burgundy `#5A202B`, charcoal text. One accent per post. |
| Fonts | Cormorant Garamond for headlines, DM Sans for small text. No more than 2 fonts on any post. |
| Layout | Lots of empty space. One message per slide. Text never covers a face or the invitation art. |
| Format | Feed posts 4:5 (1080×1350). Reels 9:16 (1080×1920), with text inside the safe area (no text in the bottom 20%). |
| Motion | Slow, smooth reveals, the same feel as the website. No flashy transitions. |
| Grid | Alternate a Reel (phone screen with invitation art), then a carousel (ivory background), then a Reel. Pin 3 posts. |
| Voice | Warm and precise: "Every ritual. Every name. Every blessing." Short sentences, a little Hindi, no "OMG" or "best in India". |

## 4. Content pillars (what we post, and how often)

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **The reveal** (shows the product) | 40% | Screen recordings of each opening: wax seal, doors, diya, marigold curtain, monogram. "Tap to reveal" moments. |

| **Behind the scenes** (builds trust) | 15% | Names typed into a design, a before-and-after of a family's draft wording, Hindi spell-check. |
| **Prices and process** (sells) | 10% | "What ₹3,999 gets you", "From order to link in 48 hours", video packages. |

**Cadence for the first 30 days:** 4 Reels and 2 carousels a week, plus Stories every day (polls, "which design?", behind the scenes). After 30 days, keep what gets the most shares and saves.

## 6. The first Reel, ready to shoot

**Idea:** the wax seal breaks and the invitation opens, recorded on a real phone so it feels like a guest receiving it.

- **Length:** 9 to 12 seconds, so it gets rewatched.
- **Audio:** the demo's own shehnai (sound on), or a calm trending instrumental from Instagram's library. Avoid copyrighted film songs.
- **Shot list:**
  1. **0 to 1.5 s:** a WhatsApp chat on a phone. The message "Aarohi & Vivaan invite you 💌" arrives with a link, and a thumb taps it.
  2. **1.5 to 4 s:** the burgundy envelope fills the screen and the "Tap to Reveal" text breathes.
  3. **4 to 6 s:** the thumb taps the wax seal; the seal breaks and the flap opens.
  4. **6 to 8 s:** the golden swirl into the card (Ganesh line, names); gold leaves start falling.
  5. **8 to 11 s:** quick scroll: scratch to reveal the date, the countdown, the venue's "View on maps".
  6. **End frame, 1 second:** ivory screen with "Invitations Made Extraordinary · Bfiluxe".
- **On-screen hook (first second):** "Your guests will open THIS 👀"
- **Caption:**
  ```
  Not a PDF. Not a forward. An invitation they actually open. 💌

  Wax seal, shehnai, every ritual and a map for each function, all in one link for WhatsApp.

  This is a sample (names are fictional). Comment INVITE and we'll send you the live link.
  From ₹3,999 · Hindi & English
  ```
- **Hashtags (5 maximum):** #marwariwedding #weddinginvitation #digitalinvitation #indianwedding #einvite
- **Cover:** the moment the seal breaks, with the text "Tap to reveal".
- **How to record:** open `/live-demo-1/` on a phone with the sound on and screen-record. Record the WhatsApp opening with a real chat to your own number.

## 7. Growth tactics that cost little

- **Comment-to-DM:** "Comment INVITE for the link." Replies drive reach, and Instagram now values shares in DMs highly. Answer every DM within an hour (that's the promise on the website).
- **Send-to-a-friend prompts:** "Send this to the cousin who's doing the invitations."
- **Collaborations:**
  - Use Instagram's Collab feature with wedding planners, mehendi artists and photographers from the website's partner programme (15 to 20% commission).
  - One collab Reel a week puts us in front of their audience.
- **WhatsApp:** post every Reel to your WhatsApp Status and business catalogue. Most Indian wedding buying happens there.
- **Free card maker:** short Reels such as "Made a Griha Pravesh card in 30 seconds, free". These bring traffic all year, not only in wedding season.
- **Timing:** 7 to 10 pm on weekdays and late mornings on Sundays (families plan together). Check Instagram Insights after 2 weeks and adjust.
- **Wedding seasons:** post more from October to February and around April to May. Before Akshaya Tritiya and Dev Uthani Ekadashi, post "plan your invitations now".

## 8. What to measure (weekly, in Metricool or Insights)

| Measure | Why | Early target |
|---|---|---|
| Shares (DMs and Stories) | The strongest reach signal now | 2%+ of views |
| Saves | Shows useful content | Wording posts above average |
| 3-second hold rate | Whether the hook works | 60%+ |
| Link taps and "INVITE" comments | Buying intent | Track weekly |
| WhatsApp enquiries from Instagram | The real goal | Ask "How did you find us?" |

## 9. Rules we keep

- Samples always say the names are fictional. Never present a demo as a real client.
- No fake reviews, follower counts or "trusted by" claims.
- Real client posts only with written family permission.
- No copyrighted film music in our own Reels; use Instagram's licensed library or our original demo music.
- Don't post until the profile (name, bio, link, 3 pinned posts) is ready, so the first visitors see a finished brand.
