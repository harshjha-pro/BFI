Why Marwari only

A Marwari wedding is not a template with Indian colours. It is the Ganesh puja that opens every card, the Mayra that honours the maternal family, the Mahila sangeet, and a long, careful list of elders whose names must appear exactly right.

Generic invitation apps miss these. Printed cards are beautiful but slow, expensive to post and impossible for relatives abroad. BFI brings both worlds together: the care of a traditional kankotri, delivered as an invitation your family opens on their phones.
