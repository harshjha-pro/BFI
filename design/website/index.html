Questions

Frequently asked

How long does it take?

Basic is ready in about 5 days, Premium in about 10 and Luxury in about 21. Rush delivery is available for a 25% fee.

What do we need to send you?

Names exactly as they should appear, parents' and grandparents' names, your functions with dates, times and venues, RSVP details and a few couple photos. Our guided form makes sure nothing is missed.

How do changes work?

You send all your changes together as one revision round from your private page. Your designer works through them as a checklist. Basic includes 1 round, Premium 2 and Luxury unlimited reasonable rounds.

Can the invitation be in Hindi or Marwari?

Yes: English, Hindi, Marwari or bilingual, with traditional openings like “Shri Ganeshaya Namah” and blessings written the way your family wants.

We live abroad. Can you still help?

Absolutely. Many of our families are NRIs. Everything happens online and guests anywhere can open the invitation on their phones.
