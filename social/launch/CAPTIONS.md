## Order to post

1. **Day 1, evening (7 to 9 pm):** post the Reel first, then pin it.
2. **Day 2:** post the 3-slide carousel, then pin it.
3. **Stories, every day until launch:** reshare the Reel; add a poll ("Which colour for our first design: ivory or burgundy?"); add a "Launching soon" countdown sticker.

## Tips

- **Reel cover:** in Instagram, pick `reel-cover.png` (the "Bfiluxe · Launching soon" frame). Use "Adjust cover" to keep the grid tidy.
- **Music:** the Reel's music is original and copyright-free. You can also mute it and add a calm trending instrumental from Instagram's audio library; trending audio can help reach.
- **Order:** don't post anything else until launch. Three pinned posts (Reel, carousel and, later, the launch post) make the profile look finished.
- **Stories:** reply to every comment and DM in the first hour; early engagement helps reach.
